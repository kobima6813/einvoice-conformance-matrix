Methodology

How we test

Same input for everyone

600 synthetic shop orders per configuration (300 B2B + 300 B2C; DE, 19%/7% VAT, coupons, shipping), fed identically into every generator.

Official validators

KoSIT/XRechnung configuration, CEN EN 16931 schematron (UBL and CII), XSD schema and Peppol BIS 3.0.20 rules. Second validator: phive-rules 4.6.3 (XRechnung 3.0.2 or EN 16931 1.3.16) on all 18,898 invoices; its verdict matches KoSIT on all 12,898 invoices that have a KoSIT scenario.

Money check

Beyond the rules: we compare the amount due in the invoice with the amount actually charged for the order.
