39.7% of Factur-X invoices from a popular WooCommerce invoice plugin fail schema validation

We fed the same 600 synthetic shop orders through WooCommerce, Odoo 19 and four libraries, then checked all 9,000 invoices against KoSIT/XRechnung, CEN EN 16931, Peppol BIS 3.0.20 and the amount actually charged. The rule sets often disagree.
