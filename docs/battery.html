Free self-check · one battery passport · runs in your browser

Check one EU battery passport

Paste or open one battery passport in the Battery Pass data model JSON format (v1.2.0), optionally with the QR code's GS1 Digital Link. It is checked locally in your browser and is never uploaded.

Unofficial pre-check, not a certification. It does not replace conformity assessment, a notified body, or legal advice. Several EU acts it refers to are still drafts.

What this checks — and what it does not

  • Structure (final model): each of the seven Battery Pass aspects against the Battery Pass Data Model v1.2.0 schema (aligned with DIN DKE SPEC 99100 and Annex XIII of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542): required fields, types, allowed values, identifier patterns, ranges.
  • Consistency (final requirements): rated energy vs rated capacity × nominal voltage, energy reported in Wh instead of kWh, min < nominal < max voltage, percentages within 0–100, remaining capacity/energy not above rated, manufacturing and update dates not in the future, put into service after manufacture, CAS check digits, chemistry vs declared materials, total material mass vs battery mass, industrial batteries ≤ 2 kWh (outside Art. 77), e-mail addresses.
  • Draft checks (methodology or format acts not yet adopted): carbon footprint stages complete and summing to the total, absolute footprint vs per-kWh value × lifetime energy, footprint declared per kWh of rated energy instead of per kWh delivered over life (Annex II), performance class format; recycled content shares and duplicates, renewable content range; GS1 Digital Link syntax, GTIN check digit, serial per battery, serial character set, link vs product identifier (GS1 Digital Link is one permitted identifier scheme under EN 18219/18220, not the only one).
  • Not checked: whether the values are true, access rights per Annex XIII parts 2–4, the passport's hosting/API (EN 18222), signatures, the EU registry, or the QR image itself. A clean result does not mean the passport complies.

Test corpus (2 valid + 33 broken passports) and rule list: docs/battery on GitHub.
