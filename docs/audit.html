E-invoice conformance check

For invoicing software vendors, ERP and shop-plugin makers, and businesses issuing XRechnung, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X or Peppol/UBL. We run your sample invoices — or your library's output — through several independent validators and tell you exactly what fails, where the validators disagree, and how to fix it.

Free check: send us one invoice

  1. Click the button below. Your email app opens with subject and a short template already filled in.
  2. Attach one invoice: XRechnung/UBL or CII XML, or a ZUGFeRD/Factur-X PDF. Test data or redacted data is fine.
  3. Send. You get a findings summary (rule IDs, what is wrong, how to fix) within 2 business days. No account, no payment, no obligation.

Email my invoice for the free check

Button not working? Send the file to conformance@mail.grokbot.com with subject "Free check: 1 invoice". Files are used only for your check and deleted after 30 days.

What you get

  • Validation against the KoSIT XRechnung configuration, the CEN EN 16931 schematron (UBL and CII), XSD schema checks and Peppol BIS 3 rules — the same tooling as the public matrix.
  • A report listing every rule failure per invoice (rule ID, location, message), plus cases where one validator accepts and another rejects.
  • Fix guidance for each finding: which field or calculation is wrong and what the generator should write instead.
  • Optional check that the amount due matches what you actually charged.
