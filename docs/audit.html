FAQ

Which formats do you check?

XRechnung (UBL and CII), ZUGFeRD / Factur-X (PDF with embedded XML, EN 16931 profiles) and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 UBL.

Do you fix my software?

We tell you exactly what to change and re-check after your fix (audit package). Changes to your code are made by you or your developer.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. A passing check means the listed validators accepted the documents at the time of testing.

What happens to my files?

They are used only for your check and deleted after 30 days. Please send test or anonymised data.

Are you affiliated with KoSIT?

No. This is an independent project using the publicly available official validation artefacts.
